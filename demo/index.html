No authority receives these reports; data is synthetic.

Every report, point and photo below was generated by demo/tools/make_demo_data.sh and pushed through the real backend on a laptop. The photos are computer-drawn road surfaces (no people, vehicles or plates). The map boxes are the repo's hand-drawn PLACEHOLDER polygons, not official boundaries. The "authority" that marks reports in progress or resolved is the same script holding a test token. No email was sent: SMTP was unset and the digest is a dry run.

PotholeAlertBD: what it does, shown with fake data

A Flutter app and a Node backend for reporting potholes in Bangladesh with a photo and GPS point. Its audit log, judge.md, reached Update 17 of hardening, but no city desk, NGO or government channel has agreed to receive the reports. This page shows the backend's real behaviour on synthetic input, so the work can be seen without pretending anyone acts on it. LEARNINGS.md explains what was learned.
